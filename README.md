# Simple Piano

A small piano you can play right in your browser. Click or tap the keys, or use your computer keyboard. Choose an octave and adjust the volume to your liking.

## Song presets

Choose a song and press **Play** to hear it. Press **Stop** to stop playback. Presets include:

- Twinkle Twinkle

- Mary Had a Little Lamb

- Ode to Joy

- Happy Birthday

## Run

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No installation or extra software is needed. Sound starts after you interact with a key or press **Play**.
